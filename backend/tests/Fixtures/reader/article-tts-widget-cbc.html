Authorities have confirmed the death of a third attendee of the Burning Man festival, days after organizers said two other festival-goers had died during the annual event in the Nevada desert. The gathering draws tens of thousands of people to a temporary city built on a dry lake bed each year.

The man, who was from California, died on his way to hospital after a medical emergency, according to a statement from the local sheriff's office. Investigators said there was no indication that the three deaths were connected, and each remained under review as the festival wound down over the weekend.

Organizers urged participants to stay hydrated and to watch out for one another in the harsh desert conditions, where daytime heat and fine alkaline dust place a heavy strain on the body across the week.
