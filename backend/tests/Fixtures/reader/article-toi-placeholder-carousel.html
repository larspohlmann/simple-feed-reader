An 82-year-old tea importer ordered to leave his compound

An 82-year-old California tea importer spent 53 years building a two-acre compound with 36 separate structures, adding to it through decades of permit disputes with the county that never reached a settlement either side accepted.

The property drew a 2024 listing on the National Register of Historic Places, a recognition that arrived in the same year a court reviewed the outstanding violations and weighed whether the structures could remain where they stood.

After the hearing the court ordered the importer to leave the compound, ending a dispute that had run for the better part of his adult life and leaving the future of the hand-built structures to the county and the new owners.
