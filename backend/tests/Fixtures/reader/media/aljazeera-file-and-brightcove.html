Harry Kane scores goal by winning Golden Shoe for the second time

English footballer Harry Kane won the title of Europe's best goalscorer, awarded the Golden Shoe for the second time, after a season in which he scored more league goals than any other player on the continent and carried his club to the title.

The striker, who moved to Germany two summers ago, collected the trophy in Munich on Tuesday and said the award belonged to the whole squad, which had supplied the chances he turned into the goals that decided the race.
