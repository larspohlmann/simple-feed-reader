Your feeds, laid out like a magazine.

An RSS/Atom reader you run yourself — for you alone or for several users. Articles, podcasts, YouTube channels, Bluesky and Mastodon, in one calm reading column.

Three ways to read

Magazine, two-pane, or compact list.

Switch any time. The magazine gives the lead story the big image; the two-pane view keeps the article beside the list with a draggable split; the list is one line per entry for working through a lot fast.

Everything a reader should do

Read, listen, watch — and keep up with the people you follow.

Reader view

The extracted full text with images, audio and video inline. Reading-time estimates, a progress bar, and a focus mode that dims everything but the passage in front of you.

Search you can keep

Full-text search with exact phrases. Save a search as a named view in the sidebar, each with its own unread count — and get a daily or weekly email digest of new matches.

Podcasts & YouTube

A persistent player with a reorderable playlist. Paste an Apple Podcasts, SoundCloud or YouTube link into “Add feed” and the reader subscribes to the show or channel.

Bluesky & Mastodon

Connect an account and its home timeline, lists, custom feeds and follows arrive as feeds. Posts show what they embed; likes and replies stay in the reader.

“For you”, optional

A model of your choice ranks your unread articles by what you favorite, keep and read. Any OpenAI-compatible API — including local models via Ollama or LM Studio — with a cost overview per run.

Feeds, tidy

Paste a site address and the app finds the feed. Preview before you subscribe, import and export OPML, tag with colours and icons, and see feed health in plain language.

Built for the phone too

The same reader, in your pocket.

A collapsible sidebar with swipe gestures, pull-to-refresh, touch-friendly controls, and a player bar that follows you.

Your instance, your rules

Private by construction.

  • Sign in your way. Email and password, Google or Apple, or a passkey. Registration is protected by an invisible proof-of-work challenge — no CAPTCHA.
  • You are the admin. Approve, suspend or delete users; optional trial periods and per-user feed limits; SMTP and outbound proxy settings with a built-in test.
  • Your data leaves when you say. Export the whole account to a backup file and restore it on another instance, or delete it entirely.
  • AI stays optional and local if you like. Recommendations run against the provider you pick — your own machine included.

Run it

One command. Your own instance.

You need Docker and git. The installer clones the project, checks out the latest release, generates the secrets it can, asks for the things only you know, and starts the stack.

# read it first: scripts/install.sh — the installer never deletes data
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/larspohlmann/simple-feed-reader/main/scripts/install.sh | bash
