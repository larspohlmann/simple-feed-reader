# Screenshots

A tour of simple-feed-reader, taken on a demo account that follows a handful of
mainstream news, tech, science, food, culture and sport feeds. Every surface
works in a light and a dark theme; both are shown where they differ most.

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

## Reading

### The magazine

The default layout: a single reading column of varied cards, with the sidebar of
feeds, tags, and saved searches. The lead card gets the big image.

![Magazine layout, light theme](screenshots/magazine-light.webp)

![Magazine layout, dark theme](screenshots/magazine-dark.webp)

The airy style drops the card frames and lets the images breathe.

![Magazine layout in the airy style, dark theme](screenshots/magazine-airy-dark.webp)

### The two-pane view

The entry list on the left, the article next to it. Drag the divider to change
the split.

![Two-pane view with the reader pane, light theme](screenshots/pane-light.webp)

![Two-pane view with the reader pane, dark theme](screenshots/pane-dark.webp)

### The compact list

One line per entry with a thumbnail, for working through a lot of items fast.

![Compact list layout](screenshots/list-light.webp)

### The reader view

The extracted full text, with author, reading-time estimate and a link to the
original. Switch to the feed's own version at any time.

![Reader view, dark theme](screenshots/reader-dark.webp)

### Search and saved searches

Full-text search across your articles, with matches highlighted. Save a search
as a named view in the sidebar; each keeps its own unread count.

![Search results for "climate"](screenshots/search-light.webp)

### Kept

Alongside favorites, a separate list for the articles you want to come back to.

![The kept list](screenshots/kept-light.webp)

## "For you"

An optional personal feed. A model of your choice ranks your unread entries
against what you favorite, keep and read.

![The "For you" recommendations](screenshots/for-you-light.webp)
