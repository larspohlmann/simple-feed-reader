# Your stream Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the For you stream into **Your stream**, a stream that works without AI. Its word list is built
locally from the reader's favourite, kept, liked and shared articles and their saved searches. The stream gets its
own module, settings section, settings row and inclusion switch.

**Architecture:**
- The AI term path is deleted first. The stored stems keep matching in the meantime.
- The non-AI stream code moves from `Service/Curation/Recommendation/Stream` into a new module,
  `Service/Curation/YourStream`, which never names `Curation/Recommendation` or `Curation/Ai`. It is renamed
  `YourStream*`.
- The wire and the frontend are renamed. A new `InclusionSurface::YourStream` takes over every stream read.
- A local builder picks the stems that stand out against the account's 30-day background. An hourly check rebuilds
  the list when its inputs change, and a daily rebuild runs regardless. The new `your_stream_word` and
  `your_stream_state` tables hold the result.
- The switch and the precision leave `RecommendationSettings` for a `your_stream_settings` row. That row is served
  by `GET`/`PUT /api/me/your-stream` and has a settings section of its own.

**Tech Stack:** Symfony 8.1 / Doctrine ORM (PHP 8.5), MySQL + SQLite migrations, Angular 20 standalone + signals,
Transloco (de/en/fr/es), Jest, PHPUnit 12.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-your-stream-design.md` (issue #2601). Prior plans for vocabulary:
`2026-11-27-for-you-stream.md` (#2582) and `2026-11-28-for-you-stream-settings.md` (#2594). The pre-flight scan
is `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-28-2601-your-stream/preflight.md`, and its rulings are applied below.

## Global Constraints

- **Precision steps** keep their names and thresholds. Narrow → wide, the minimum scores are **4.0, 3.0, 2.0, 1.5,
  1.0**. The default is step 4 (`wide`, 1.5).
- **Word-list sources:** favourites, kept, liked and shared, from `ReadingHistoryRepository`. Each entry counts only
  in its highest section, and viewed entries are **not** a source. Take the **200 newest per section**, only from
  the stream's own feeds, plus the saved-search terms.
- **Selection:**
  - A stem needs **≥ 2 interest articles**. Keep the top **60 per language**.
  - Weights use the AI's old stored scale, **0.1–1.0**: `InterestTermParser` divided the AI's 1–10 by 10, and the
    builder writes the same range.
  - Headline stems weigh **1.0** and text stems **0.5**. A German compound part counts **0.5** of its term
    (`PART_WEIGHT_SHARE`).
- **Rebuild:** check each account **at most once an hour**. Rebuild it when its input fingerprint changed, and
  **once a day regardless**.
- **Data:** stream data exists only on develop/Strato, so tables can be renamed or rebuilt freely. **The membership
  rows and the sweep mark must survive.** The AI term sets, phrases and term runs are dropped.
- **Module boundary:** the new module never names `App\Service\Curation\Recommendation\…` or
  `App\Service\Curation\Ai\…`. `ServiceModuleBoundaryRule` is **not** extended (memory: no rule edits unasked).

## Decisions taken while planning (spec left them open)

- **Order: delete the AI path before the move.** Moving first would leave `InterestTermRefresh` and friends in
  `Recommendation` while they depend on the new module, and `LlmInterestTermExtractor` would implement a
  `YourStream` interface. If the AI path goes first, the stored stems stay, and the sweep keeps matching on them
  until the local builder replaces them (Task 7).
- **No separate rarity refresh.** The daily rebuild reads the same 30-day background, so the builder writes each
  word's rarity together with the word.
  - `raritiesRefreshedAt`, `findWithStaleRarities` and `YourStreamBackground::refreshRarities` go away in Task 6.
  - `YourStreamWordSelection` is the only home of the rarity formula and of the 500-entry floor.
- **Selection formula.** For language key L, let A be the interest articles in L, plus 3 per saved-search term
  counted in L. For a stem s:
  - `support` = the sum of its position weights over articles (1.0 headline, 0.5 text), plus 3 × its share for each
    search term.
  - p_int = (support + 0.5)/(A + 1) and p_bg = (df_bg + 0.5)/(max(N, 500) + 1).
  - Keep s when it appears in ≥ 2 articles (a search term counts as 3) and p_int/p_bg ≥ **2.0**.
  - Rank kept stems by `p_int × ln(p_int/p_bg)`. Keep the top 60, with ties broken by stem ascending.
  - Weight = `max(1, round(10 × score/maxScore)) / 10`.
  - Rarity = `ln((max(N, 500) + 2)/(df_bg + 1))`, today's formula.
  - Limit of the lift rule: with about 10 articles, a word that only a search contributed drops out only once its
    background share is above roughly half its interest share. That means about 12–16 % of background entries.
    `MIN_LIFT` is the lever if that proves too lenient, not the fixture.
- **Saved searches** are stemmed in each language of the stream's feeds and in the cut form (`null`), the way
  `InterestTermStems` stemmed AI phrases: a whole word counts 1.0 and a German part 0.5.
- **Display words.**
  - The surface form comes out of the stemming pipeline itself, so stems and surfaces can never drift apart.
    `CasefoldedWords` returns each casefolded word with its case-kept surface. `WordEquivalents` carries the
    surface through exact-word replacement and phrase collapsing, and `canonicalizeWords` stays its projection.
  - A stem shows the surface word it came from most often; on a tie, the first seen.
  - A stem that a saved search contributed shows the term as typed. The first search by position wins.
  - A canonical with no surface (a collapsed phrase such as `european union` → `eunion`, or an exact-word
    replacement such as `EU` → `eunion`) shows the stem.
  - The settings list de-duplicates equal words per language and keeps the highest weight.
  - Cut-form words come last, with `language: null`.
- **Fingerprint:** sha1 of the JSON of the sorted interest entry ids and the sorted saved-search terms. Changing the
  inclusion switches changes the restricted history, so the fingerprint changes with it.
- **State row.**
  - It is created on an account's first check, with its mark at the settled ceiling, so nothing is backfilled.
  - An account without words keeps its row. The sweep walks it, so the mark moves without matching, and a later
    list resumes from a recent mark.
- **Disabled accounts** are never checked or built. The sweep still moves their marks to the ceiling unmatched, as
  #2594 decided, so re-enabling does not backfill. This deviates from the wording of spec §4; see PR notes.
- **Tables:**
  - `your_stream_word`.
  - `your_stream_state`.
  - `your_stream_entry`, rebuilt from `for_you_stream_entry`.
  - `your_stream_settings`, with columns `enabled` and `match_precision`. `PRECISION` is a reserved word in MySQL.
- **Wire extras:** the GET also carries `defaultPrecision`, as today, because the stepper's reset uses it.
- **Backup.**
  - `YourStreamSettings` is not backed up, like `RecommendationSettings`; `docs/backup.md` says so.
  - The inclusion switch is backed up.
  - The backup lines carry an `InclusionSwitches` value instead of a fifth bool, because `SubscriptionLine` already
    has 10 constructor parameters.
- **Test seam:** the word sweep depends on `YourStreamWordBuilderInterface`, a consumer-owned interface in the
  `YourStreamWordBuilder/` folder. A sweep test can hand in a failing builder without a non-final hack.
- **Measurement (Task 10)** ships a read-only `app:your-stream:explain` command. The baseline is the number of
  matches per day while the AI list was live, from the membership rows, which survive every migration here.

## PR notes (copy into the PR body)

- **Deviation from spec §4.** A disabled account is never checked or built, but the sweep still moves its mark to
  the ceiling without matching.
  - Without that, re-enabling after a long pause would match the last 48 h of the off period. That is a backfill,
    which the spec rules out (#2594).
  - Moving the mark costs one UPDATE and matches nothing. Overrule if "no sweep" was meant literally.
- **Scope beyond the spec.** Saved-post feeds (`SavedFeedFactory`) and group private feeds (`PrivateFeeds`) start
  switched out of Your stream, as they do for For you. Otherwise saved and private posts would flood the stream, and
  the migration's copy-from-For-you rule already treats them that way.
- **Accepted edge case.** `ReadingHistoryRepository` decides an entry's "highest section" across all followed
  copies, whatever their inclusion surface.
  - So a post you liked, whose favourited copy sits in a feed switched out of Your stream, drops out of the
    interests, even though its liked copy is in the stream.
  - This is rare. Fixing it means restricting the copy conditions by surface.
- **Lift limit.** A word that only a saved search contributed drops out only once its background share is above
  roughly half its interest share: about 12–16 % of background entries for a 10-article list. `MIN_LIFT` (2.0) is
  the lever.
- **Measurement.** The results from Task 10: the baseline, both variants and the decision.
