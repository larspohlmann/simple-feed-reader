# Your stream Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn the For you stream into **Your stream**, a stream that works without AI. Its word list is built
locally from the reader's favourite, kept, liked and shared articles and their saved searches. It gets its own
module, settings section, settings row and inclusion switch.

**Architecture:** The AI term path is deleted first, and the stored stems keep matching in the meantime. Next, the
non-AI stream code moves from `Service/Curation/Recommendation/Stream` into a new module
`Service/Curation/YourStream`, which never names `Curation/Recommendation` or `Curation/Ai`, and is renamed
`YourStream*`. Then the wire and the frontend are renamed. A new `InclusionSurface::YourStream` takes over every
stream read. A local builder picks the stems that stand out against the account's 30-day background. An hourly
check rebuilds the list when its inputs change, and the list is rebuilt at least once a day. The new
`your_stream_word` / `your_stream_state` tables hold the result. The switch and the precision leave
`RecommendationSettings` for a `your_stream_settings` row, served by `GET`/`PUT /api/me/your-stream`, with a
settings section of its own.

**Tech Stack:** Symfony 8.1 / Doctrine ORM (PHP 8.5), MySQL + SQLite migrations, Angular 20 standalone + signals,
Transloco (de/en/fr/es), Jest, PHPUnit 12.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-your-stream-design.md` (issue #2601). Prior plans for vocabulary:
`2026-11-27-for-you-stream.md` (#2582), `2026-11-28-for-you-stream-settings.md` (#2594).

## Global Constraints

- The precision steps keep their names and thresholds. Narrow to wide, the minimum scores are **4.0, 3.0, 2.0,
  1.5, 1.0**, and the default is step 4 (`wide`, 1.5).
- The word list's sources: **favourites, kept, liked, shared** (`ReadingHistoryRepository`, each entry only in its
  highest section, **not viewed**), **200 newest per section**, only from the stream's own feeds, plus the
  saved-search terms.
- Selection: a stem must appear in **≥ 2 interest articles**, the top **60 per language** are kept, and weights
  land on the AI's old scale. The AI's 1–10 was stored as **0.1–1.0** (`InterestTermParser` divided by 10), and the
  builder writes the same 0.1–1.0. Headline stems weigh **1.0**, text stems **0.5**, and German compound parts
  **0.5** of their term (`PART_WEIGHT_SHARE`).
- Rebuild: an account is checked **at most once an hour**. It is rebuilt when its input fingerprint changed, and
  **once a day regardless**.
- The stream data is develop/Strato-only. Tables can be renamed and rebuilt freely, and the **membership rows and
  the sweep mark survive**. The AI term sets, phrases and term runs are dropped.
- The new module never names `App\Service\Curation\Recommendation\…` or `App\Service\Curation\Ai\…`.

## Decisions taken while planning (spec left them open)

- **Order: delete the AI path before the move.** Moving first would leave `InterestTermRefresh` and friends in
  `Recommendation` while they depend on the new module, and `LlmInterestTermExtractor` would implement a
  `YourStream` interface. That is a cross-module tangle with no purpose. Deleting first leaves the stored stems in
  place. The sweep keeps matching on them until the local builder replaces them (Task 7), so the stream never goes
  dark on dev.
- **No separate rarity refresh.** The daily rebuild reads the same 30-day background the rarity came from, so the
  builder writes each word's rarity with the word. `raritiesRefreshedAt` and `findWithStaleRarities` go away.
- **Selection formula.** For language key L, A = the interest articles in L plus 3 per saved-search term counted in
  L. For a stem s: `support` = the sum of its position weights over articles (1.0 headline, 0.5 text) plus 3 × its
  share for each search term. p_int = (support + 0.5)/(A + 1), and p_bg = (df_bg + 0.5)/(max(N, 500) + 1). The stem
  is kept when it appears in ≥ 2 articles (a search term counts as 3) and p_int/p_bg ≥ **2.0**. It ranks by
  `p_int × ln(p_int/p_bg)`, and the top 60 stay (ties broken by stem, ascending). Weight =
  `max(1, round(10 × score/maxScore)) / 10`. Rarity = `ln((max(N, 500) + 2)/(df_bg + 1))`, today's formula.
- **Saved searches** are stemmed in each language of the stream's feeds and in the cut form (`null`), the way
  `InterestTermStems` stemmed AI phrases (whole word 1.0, German parts 0.5).
- **Display words.** A stem shows the case-kept surface word it came from most often (ties: first seen). A stem
  that a saved search contributed shows the term as typed (first search by position wins). A phrase canonical with
  no surface word (`onu`, `eunion`) shows the stem. The settings list de-duplicates equal words per language and
  keeps the highest weight. Cut-form words form a last group with `language: null`.
- **Fingerprint.** sha1 of the JSON of the sorted interest entry ids and the sorted saved-search terms. Changing the
  inclusion switches changes the restricted history, and the fingerprint with it.
- **State row.** It is created on an account's first check, and its mark starts at the settled ceiling (no
  backfill). An account without words keeps its row, so the sweep walks it and moves its mark without matching. A
  later list therefore resumes from a recent mark.
- **Disabled accounts** are never checked or built. The sweep still moves their marks to the ceiling unmatched, as
  #2594 decided, so a re-enable does not backfill. Moving a mark costs nothing and matches nothing.
- **Tables:** `your_stream_word`, `your_stream_state` and `your_stream_entry` (rebuilt from the old ones),
  `your_stream_settings` (columns `enabled` and `match_precision`, because `PRECISION` is a MySQL reserved word).
- **Wire extras.** The GET body also carries `defaultPrecision`, as today, because the stepper's reset uses it. The
  spec's `{enabled, precision, words}` is a subset of that body.
- **Backup.** `YourStreamSettings` is not backed up, like `RecommendationSettings` (documented in `docs/backup.md`).
  The inclusion switch is backed up as the spec says.
- **Backup lines carry `InclusionSwitches`** instead of a fifth bool. `SubscriptionLine` is at 10 constructor
  parameters, and an 11th trips PHPMD's `ExcessiveParameterList`. One value object also ends the four-times-repeated
  `includeIn…` reads.
- **Measurement (Task 10)** ships a read-only `app:your-stream:explain` command. The baseline is the matches per day
  in `your_stream_entry` for the dev account while the AI list was live, and the membership rows survive every
  migration here.

## Review Focus

1. **A favourite from a feed switched out of Your stream** must not shape the list. Pinned in Task 6
   (`testAnArticleFromAFeedOutsideYourStreamIsNotAnInterest`).
2. **A generic word from a multi-word saved search** ("climate change" → `chang`) must not enter the list unless it
   stands out. Pinned in Task 6 (`testAGenericSearchWordThatIsCommonInTheBackgroundIsNotKept`).
3. **A backup written before this change** (no `includeInYourStream`, no `yourStreamExcludedNetworks`) must restore
   the For you values into the new switch. Pinned in Task 4 (`testAnOlderLineTakesYourStreamFromForYou`,
   `testAnOlderAccountLineExcludesFromYourStreamWhatForYouExcluded`).
4. **A list that goes empty** must keep the sidebar row while members exist, and must keep the sweep mark. Pinned in
   Task 7 (`testAnEmptyBuildKeepsTheStateRowAndItsMark`) and Task 8
   (`testAnEnabledStreamWithoutWordsButWithMembersStaysAvailable`).
5. **An unchanged input set** must still be rebuilt after 24 h (background drift), but not within the hour. Pinned
   in Task 7 (`testAnUnchangedAccountIsRebuiltAfterADay`, `testAnAccountCheckedWithinTheHourIsNotCheckedAgain`).
