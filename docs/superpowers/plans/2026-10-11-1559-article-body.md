# #1559 ReaderViewComponent: the article-body DOM wiring in a provided helper

**Goal:** an `ArticleBody` helper, provided and connected like `ArticleGestures`,
`ArticleScrollRestore` and `ReadingScope`, owns the wiring on the rendered article body:
decorating after render, the capturing error listener (image proxy, video fallback) and
the `t` cinema key. The component constructor keeps its connect calls and the
entry-change effect. Behaviour does not change.

## Rulings

- `ArticleBody.connect({ content, html, pageUrl, enclosureUrl, decorated })`: the body
  takes the rendered HTML as a signal instead of injecting `ArticleSource`, so its spec
  needs no source double. `decorated(host)` is the hand-off the issue names: the
  component sets the TOC, refreshes the reading scope and re-seats the scroll restore.
- The effects are created in `connect`, at the same point in the component's effect
  order as before (after `scope.connect`, before `scope.observeResizes`), so the
  decoration still runs after `[innerHTML]` lands and before `restore.reseat`.
- The video-fallback labels are translated by the helper's own `TranslocoService`, the
  instance it hands `decorateArticle`; the component no longer injects it.
- `ImageProxyService` stays resolved lazily on the first failed image, as today.
- The `t` key moves from the component's `(document:keydown)` host binding to a
  document listener the helper adds and removes on destroy.
- Toolbar measuring (`reserveToolbarHeight`) is already outside the constructor and
  stays: the issue's proposal does not name it.
- Feed-video handling stays where it is; #1558 moves it.
